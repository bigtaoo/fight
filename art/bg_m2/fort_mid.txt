Paint on this blank sheet of paper a long palisade of sharpened wooden logs bound with rope running across the whole width of the picture, broken by a wooden watchtower on stilts and two flagpoles with tattered cloth banners, all rising from the bottom edge of the picture. Everything above the palisade is left as empty plain paper. Traditional Chinese sumi-e brush painting with wet ink washes on pale rice paper, like a Song dynasty handscroll: soft bleeding washes and dry-brush strokes, no pencil lines, no hatching, no outlines drawn with a pen. Light and mid grey ink with a little pale indigo, no pure black areas. Flat side view with no perspective and no ground plane: the objects stand directly on the bottom edge of the picture and the paper around them stays blank. No grid, no panels, no frame, no border, no people, no animals, no text, no seal.
