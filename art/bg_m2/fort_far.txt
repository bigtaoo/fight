Paint on this blank sheet of paper the far background layer of a mountain bandit fortress scene for a side-scrolling action game: misty mountain slopes across the whole width of the upper half, and on them the distant walls of a wooden fortress, a long palisade of sharpened logs, a few wooden watchtowers and tall flagpoles with tattered banners, all faint and pale with distance in mid grey ink wash. The lower third of the picture stays empty plain paper, where the characters will walk. Chinese ink wash painting on pale rice paper: light and mid grey ink with a little pale indigo, soft washes and dry-brush edges, no pure black areas, no strong contrast. One single seamless painting: no grid, no panels, no frame, no border, no people, no animals, no text, no seal.
