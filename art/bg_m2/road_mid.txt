Paint on this blank sheet of paper a long row of gnarled pine trees, weathered boulders and low shrubs spread evenly across the whole width of the picture, some tall, some short, with gaps between them, every one rising from the bottom edge of the picture. Everything above and between them is left as empty plain paper. Traditional Chinese sumi-e brush painting with wet ink washes on pale rice paper, like a Song dynasty handscroll: soft bleeding washes and dry-brush strokes, no pencil lines, no hatching, no outlines drawn with a pen. Light and mid grey ink with a little pale indigo, no pure black areas. Flat side view with no perspective and no ground plane: the objects stand directly on the bottom edge of the picture and the paper around them stays blank. No grid, no panels, no frame, no border, no people, no animals, no text, no seal.
