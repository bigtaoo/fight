Paint on this blank sheet of paper a row of thick round red-brown wooden pillars on stone bases spread evenly across the whole width of the picture, with paper lanterns hanging from a beam between them, rising from the bottom edge of the picture, in indigo-grey ink wash with a touch of dull red. Everything between the pillars is left as empty plain paper. Traditional Chinese sumi-e brush painting with wet ink washes on pale rice paper, like a Song dynasty handscroll: soft bleeding washes and dry-brush strokes, no pencil lines, no hatching, no outlines drawn with a pen. Light and mid grey ink with a little pale indigo, no pure black areas. Flat side view with no perspective and no ground plane: the objects stand directly on the bottom edge of the picture and the paper around them stays blank. No grid, no panels, no frame, no border, no people, no animals, no text, no seal.
