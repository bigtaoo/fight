A traditional Chinese ink wash painting in the style of a Song dynasty handscroll, sumi-e, painted with wet bleeding grey ink washes and expressive dry-brush strokes, no outlines, no pencil, no hatching. Inside a great timber hall at night: a row of four thick round lacquered wooden pillars on carved stone bases spaced evenly across the whole width, each running from the bottom edge to the top edge, joined at the top by a heavy beam, with round paper lanterns hanging from the beam between them. Between the pillars is blank pale paper. Flat side view, indigo-grey ink wash with dull red on the pillars and warm pale yellow in the lanterns, no pure black, no people, no animals, no text, no calligraphy, no seal, no signature, no frame, no border.
