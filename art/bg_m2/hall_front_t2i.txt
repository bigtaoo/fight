A traditional Chinese ink wash painting in the style of a Song dynasty handscroll, sumi-e, painted with wet bleeding grey ink washes and expressive dry-brush strokes, no outlines, no pencil, no hatching. Three separate objects close to the viewer, standing on the bottom edge with wide blank gaps between them: a big round clay wine jar sealed with red cloth, a low wooden bench, a bronze brazier with faint smoke. Nothing else in the picture: no trees, no branches, no plants, no wall. The rest of the picture is blank pale rice paper. Wide panoramic composition, flat side view, indigo-grey ink, no pure black, no people, no animals, no text, no calligraphy, no seal, no signature, no frame, no border.
