Paint on this blank sheet of paper a few foreground plants for a side-scrolling action game, close to the viewer and spread across the width with wide empty gaps between them: tall wild grass tufts, a fern, a broken branch, a low rock, all rising from the bottom edge of the picture, in mid grey ink with loose wet washes and blurred soft edges. Everything else is left as empty plain paper. Chinese ink wash painting on pale rice paper: light and mid grey ink with a little pale indigo, soft washes and dry-brush edges, no pure black areas, no strong contrast. One single seamless painting: no grid, no panels, no frame, no border, no people, no animals, no text, no seal.
