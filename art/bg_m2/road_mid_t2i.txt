A traditional Chinese shan shui ink wash painting in the style of a Song dynasty handscroll, sumi-e: a long row of gnarled pine trees with clustered needle masses, mossy boulders and low shrubs standing along the bottom edge, painted with wet bleeding grey ink washes and expressive dry-brush strokes, no outlines, no pencil, no hatching. The upper half is blank pale rice paper. Wide panoramic composition, flat side view, light and mid grey ink with a hint of pale indigo, no pure black, no people, no buildings, no text, no seal, no frame.
