A traditional Chinese ink wash painting in the style of a Song dynasty handscroll, sumi-e, painted with wet bleeding grey ink washes and expressive dry-brush strokes, no outlines, no pencil, no hatching. The back wall of a great timber hall of a mountain bandit chief at night, seen straight on and filling the whole picture: wooden wall panels and lattice windows, a low raised dais in the middle with a large wooden chair draped in a tiger pelt, two long blank cloth banners hanging down, two tall candle stands with a warm glow. The lower quarter is a plain pale floor. Night shown with soft indigo-grey washes of medium brightness, never black, no people, no animals, no text, no calligraphy, no seal, no signature, no frame, no border.
