A traditional Chinese ink wash painting in the style of a Song dynasty handscroll, sumi-e, painted with wet bleeding grey ink washes and expressive dry-brush strokes, no outlines, no pencil, no hatching. Three separate objects close to the viewer, standing on the bottom edge with wide blank gaps between them: a broken leaning wooden stake, two stacked wooden barrels, a clump of tall grass, loose wet washes with blurred soft edges. The rest of the picture is blank pale rice paper. Wide panoramic composition, flat side view, mid grey ink, no pure black, no people, no animals, no text, no calligraphy, no seal, no signature, no frame, no border.
