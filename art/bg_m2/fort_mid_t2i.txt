A traditional Chinese ink wash painting in the style of a Song dynasty handscroll, sumi-e, painted with wet bleeding grey ink washes and expressive dry-brush strokes, no outlines, no pencil, no hatching. A long wooden palisade of a mountain bandit fortress runs along the bottom edge across the whole width: tall sharpened log stakes bound with rope, clearly separate logs with pointed tops, a wooden watchtower on stilts with a small roof, and two tall flagpoles with tattered cloth banners. The upper half is blank pale rice paper. Wide panoramic composition, flat side view, light and mid grey ink with a little pale ochre, no pure black, no people, no animals, no text, no calligraphy, no seal, no signature, no frame, no border.
