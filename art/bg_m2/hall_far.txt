Paint on this blank sheet of paper the back wall of a great wooden hall of a bandit chief at night, the far background layer of a side-scrolling action game, filling the whole picture: dark timber beams and a lattice of wooden panels in indigo-grey ink wash, a raised dais in the middle with a large chair draped in a tiger pelt, big blank cloth banners hanging down on both sides, warm faint glow of candle light. The lower third of the picture is a plain pale floor area, nearly empty. Night is shown with soft indigo-grey washes of medium brightness, never black. Chinese ink wash painting on pale rice paper, soft washes and dry-brush edges, no pure black areas. One single seamless painting: no grid, no frame, no border, no people, no animals, no text, no calligraphy, no seal.
