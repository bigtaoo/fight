A single golden pheasant drawn as a 3D game creature model reference, in the style of a Chinese gongbi bird painting corrupted by black ink. Side three-quarter view, the bird standing proud on its two legs, chest out, head raised, wings folded neatly against the body, a very long tail of a few long feathers held together in one bunch, sweeping straight out behind it and slightly down, nearly level with the back, clear empty space under the tail. The whole body, neck, wings and head are grey-black ink washes with fine gongbi feather lines and pale ink edges, no other colour on the body. The ONLY colour is the long tail: every tail feather is a vivid saturated cinnabar vermilion red with fine dark ink bars, bright red from the base of the tail to the tip, plus a small vermilion red crest on the head. Long strong grey legs, clawed feet flat. Whole bird visible from beak to tail tip, centered, filling the frame. Soft even lighting, no strong shadows. Plain pure white background, no loose or floating feathers, no rock, no ground, no shadow, no ink splashes, no text, no seal, only one bird.
