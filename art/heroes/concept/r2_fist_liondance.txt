Character concept art for a 2.5D side-scrolling action game, one character only, full body from head to feet, in a dynamic ready stance turned three-quarters toward the viewer, centered, filling the frame vertically. A strong, simple, instantly readable silhouette, crisp and saturated, clean readable colour blocks. Plain pale rice paper background with only a faint grey ink wash behind the feet, no scenery, no text, no signature, no border, no extra views.
Style: Chinese gongbi heavy-colour painting with bold black ink outlines and flat mineral pigments.
Character: a strong young woman lion-dance fighter: she wears a giant colourful southern lion-dance lion head as a helmet pushed up on top of her head, the lion head with big eyes, a mirror on its forehead, azurite blue and gold fur trim; a short blue jacket, wide blue trousers, a long shaggy lion-dance cape of blue and white fur trailing from her shoulders like a tail. Her forearms are covered by two smaller lion-head gauntlets with open jaws over her fists. Broad shoulders, muscular arms, wide low horse stance. Azurite blue dominates, gold, white and a little red.
