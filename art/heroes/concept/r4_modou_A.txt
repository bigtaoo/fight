Character concept art for a 2.5D side-scrolling action game, one character only, full body from head to feet, in a dynamic ready stance turned three-quarters toward the viewer, centered, filling the frame vertically. A strong, simple, instantly readable silhouette, crisp and saturated, clean readable colour blocks.
Style: Chinese gongbi heavy-colour painting made into a modern game character design: confident black ink brush outlines of varying thickness, flat rich mineral pigments with a slight grainy pigment texture, very light cel shading.
Plain pale rice paper background with only a faint grey ink wash behind the feet, no scenery, no text, no signature, no seal, no border, no extra views.
Character: Modou, a living Chinese wooden marionette gunner, like a carved Quanzhou string puppet grown to human size but with no strings: a slim jointed figure of carved honey-yellow tung-oil wood with visible mortise-and-tenon joints at the shoulders, elbows, waist and knees, wearing a short gold-and-yellow lacquered wooden armour of small plates like a Ming temple guardian, a scholar's black gauze cap carved in wood. Its face is a calm carved wooden mask with no eyes, a single thin line of glowing black ink where eyes should be. Its right forearm is a thick bronze cannon shaped like a crouching tiger with an open mouth as the muzzle; its left wooden hand holds a small carpenter's ink-line reel trailing a line of black ink. Yellow paper talismans with black ink writing tied to its arms. It looks like an ancient Chinese artefact, not a robot. Honey-yellow wood, gold lacquer and bronze dominate.
