Clean up this rough collage into one seamless, finished piece of character concept art, keeping the pose, proportions and the design of every part exactly as shown. Unify everything into one style: Chinese gongbi heavy-colour painting made into a modern game character design, confident black ink outlines of varying thickness, flat rich mineral pigments with a slight grainy texture, light cel shading, one even pale rice paper background with no patches or rectangles.
The bronze guardian-tiger head with the scroll-curl mane is the muzzle of the cannon on the right arm: join it smoothly to the end of the dark bronze barrel, repaint it in the same flat ink-outline style (not photographic), and make the mark on its forehead the simple Chinese character 王. The left hand holds the wooden ink-line reel box; remove the faded extra fingers below it, and the black ink line runs from the reel down to the ground. Keep the blank wooden mask with one ink line, the black official's hat with two long flat wings, the dougong shoulders, the lattice, the red belt, the talismans and the rocket box.
No steam, no pipes, no gears, no rivets, no text other than the 王 mark.
