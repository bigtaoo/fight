Repaint this flat layout sketch into finished character concept art for a 2.5D action game, keeping exactly the same pose, proportions, silhouette and the position of every part. Style: Chinese gongbi heavy-colour painting made into a modern game character design, confident black ink brush outlines of varying thickness, flat rich mineral pigments with a slight grainy texture, light cel shading, pale rice paper background.
The character is Modou, a living wooden automaton of ancient China, an artefact not a robot: every part is carved honey-yellow tung-oil wood with visible wood grain, joined by mortise-and-tenon joints with no nails, gold lacquer edges. The head is a smooth featureless oval wooden mask like a blank carved puppet face: no eyes, no eyebrows, no nose, no mouth, no human features at all, only one thick horizontal glowing black ink brush line across where the eyes would be. On top is a black lacquered Chinese Song-dynasty official hat (futou) with two VERY long thin straight flat wings sticking out horizontally to both sides, each wing longer than the shoulders are wide. The shoulder pieces are stacked Chinese dougong roof brackets with red ends. The chest has a carved wooden window lattice and a round bronze taotie medallion. Its right arm is a big bronze squatting-tiger cannon; the muzzle at the far end is a LARGE roaring bronze tiger head with ears, angry brows, round eyes, whiskers and fangs, the cannon bore is inside its wide-open jaws, with bronze bands and two tiger-claw legs underneath, and gamboge-yellow silk tassels. The slim left wooden hand holds a carpenter's ink-line reel (a Chinese mo dou) whose black ink line trails down to the ground in a brush stroke with ink drops. Yellow paper talismans with red and black ink writing hang from the red belt. On its back a wooden box with a small curved temple roof holds a cluster of bamboo fire-arrow rocket tubes with red tips. No steam, no pipes, no metal gears, no rivets, no glowing robot eyes, no text.
