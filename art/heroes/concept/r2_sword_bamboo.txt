Character concept art for a 2.5D side-scrolling action game, one character only, full body from head to feet, in a dynamic ready stance turned three-quarters toward the viewer, centered, filling the frame vertically. A strong, simple, instantly readable silhouette, crisp and saturated, clean readable colour blocks. Plain pale rice paper background with only a faint grey ink wash behind the feet, no scenery, no text, no signature, no border, no extra views.
Style: Chinese ink bamboo painting, sumi-e brush strokes with gongbi colour accents.
Character: a lean swordsman spirit grown from an ink bamboo painting: a human figure whose body is made of jointed green-black bamboo stalks, the joints as knees, elbows and waist, a head of a hollow bamboo hat with a cinnabar-red cord, long tapering ink bamboo leaves forming a ragged cloak and scarf that streams in the wind, a cinnabar-red tassel at the hip. Holds a thin straight sword whose blade is a single narrow bamboo leaf of polished jade-green. Ink black, malachite green and cinnabar red.
