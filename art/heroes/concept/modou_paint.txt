Repaint this flat layout sketch into finished character concept art for a 2.5D action game, keeping exactly the same pose, proportions, silhouette and the position of every part. Style: Chinese gongbi heavy-colour painting made into a modern game character design, confident black ink brush outlines of varying thickness, flat rich mineral pigments with a slight grainy texture, light cel shading, pale rice paper background.
The character is Modou, a living wooden automaton of ancient China, an artefact not a robot: every part is carved honey-yellow tung-oil wood with visible wood grain, joined by mortise-and-tenon joints with no nails, gold lacquer edges. The head is a carved wooden mask with a calm blank face and no eyes, only one glowing black ink line where the eyes should be, under a black lacquered Chinese official's hat with two long thin flat wings. The shoulder pieces are stacked Chinese dougong roof brackets with red ends. The chest has a carved wooden window lattice and a round bronze taotie medallion. Its right arm is a big bronze squatting-tiger cannon whose muzzle is a roaring tiger head, with bronze bands and two tiger-claw legs underneath, and gamboge-yellow silk tassels. The slim left wooden hand holds a carpenter's ink-line reel (a Chinese mo dou) whose black ink line trails down to the ground in a brush stroke with ink drops. Yellow paper talismans with red and black ink writing hang from the red belt. On its back a wooden box with a small curved temple roof holds a cluster of bamboo fire-arrow rocket tubes with red tips. No steam, no pipes, no metal gears, no rivets, no glowing robot eyes, no text.
