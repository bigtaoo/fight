Character concept art for a 2.5D side-scrolling action game, one character only, full body from head to feet, in a dynamic ready stance turned three-quarters toward the viewer, centered, filling the frame vertically. A strong, simple, instantly readable silhouette, crisp and saturated, clean readable colour blocks. Plain pale rice paper background with only a faint grey ink wash behind the feet, no scenery, no text, no signature, no border, no extra views.
Style: Chinese shadow puppet (piying) art: a flat jointed puppet of translucent dyed leather with intricate carved openwork patterns, lit from behind so the colours glow.
Character: a shadow-puppet gunner from a Ming dynasty shadow play, a flat side-on jointed leather puppet figure of a young dashing engineer, visible rivet joints at shoulders, elbows, hips and knees, intricate cut-out lace patterns in his robe of glowing gamboge yellow, gold and red, a black carved hat with a plume, thin dark control rods attached to his hands. He holds a long ornate Ming fire-lance musket, also a carved flat puppet piece, a puff of carved flame at its muzzle. Gamboge yellow and gold dominate.
