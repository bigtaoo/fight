Character concept art for a 2.5D side-scrolling action game, one character only, full body from head to feet, in a dynamic ready stance turned three-quarters toward the viewer, centered, filling the frame vertically. A strong, simple, instantly readable silhouette, crisp and saturated, clean readable colour blocks.
Style: Chinese gongbi heavy-colour painting made into a modern game character design: confident black ink brush outlines of varying thickness, flat rich mineral pigments with a slight grainy pigment texture, very light cel shading.
Plain pale rice paper background with only a faint grey ink wash behind the feet, no scenery, no text, no signature, no seal, no border, no extra views.
Character: a Chinese wooden automaton gunner named Modou, built entirely of joined wooden parts with mortise-and-tenon joints and no nails, medium height, in tung-oil honey-yellow wood with gold lacquer trim and bronze fittings with ancient taotie patterns. Its shoulders are stacked Chinese dougong roof brackets like pauldrons. Its head is a square wooden box with a smooth blank wooden mask face with no eyes, only a single thin glowing black ink line across where eyes would be. Glowing black ink veins run through the wood grain. Yellow paper talisman tags with black ink characters stuck on its limbs. Its right arm is a thick bronze squatting-tiger cannon with small tiger-claw braces; its left wooden hand holds a carpenter's ink-line reel (a Chinese mo dou). On its back a tall box of stacked dougong brackets that opens into a multi-tube rocket launcher. Asymmetric, stiff, precise posture. No steam, no pipes, no metal gears, no rivets. Honey-yellow wood, gold and bronze dominate, gamboge-yellow tassels.
