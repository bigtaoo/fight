A single cast-bronze Chinese guardian-tiger head ornament, seen from the side in three-quarter view facing left, like the muzzle of a Ming dynasty tiger cannon or an ancient temple bronze: square heavy jaw, wide open roaring mouth with big fangs and a dark round hole inside the mouth, fierce knotted brows, bulging round eyes, the Chinese character 王 cast on the forehead, a full ring of thick carved scroll-curl mane framing the whole face, small rounded ears, no whiskers. Dark green-gold bronze patina with gold highlights. Style: Chinese gongbi heavy-colour painting, confident black ink outlines, flat mineral pigments, light cel shading. Isolated on a plain pale rice paper background, nothing else, no body, no text.
