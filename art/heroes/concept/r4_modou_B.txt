Style: Chinese gongbi heavy-colour painting made into a modern game character design: confident black ink brush outlines of varying thickness, flat rich mineral pigments with a slight grainy pigment texture, very light cel shading.
Plain pale rice paper background with only a faint grey ink wash behind the feet, no scenery, no text, no signature, no seal, no border, no extra views.
Character: Modou, a living wooden temple guardian automaton of ancient China, built like traditional Chinese timber architecture: its shoulders and chest are stacked interlocking dougong roof brackets painted with faded red, green and gold temple patterns, its limbs are lathe-turned wooden posts joined with mortise-and-tenon joints, honey-yellow wood. On its back a small pagoda-like box of brackets with a cluster of bamboo fire-arrow rocket tubes poking out of the top. Its head is a carved wooden mask with a long hat brim like an official's hat, no eyes, one glowing ink-black line instead. One arm holds a long Ming fire-lance musket of lacquered wood with bronze bands, the other holds a carpenter's ink-line reel. Stiff upright precise posture. It looks like an ancient Chinese artefact, not a robot, no gears, no pipes, no steam. Honey-yellow wood and gold dominate, temple red and green accents.
