Character concept art for a 2.5D side-scrolling action game, one character only, full body from head to feet, in a dynamic ready stance turned three-quarters toward the viewer, centered, filling the frame vertically. A strong, simple, instantly readable silhouette, crisp and saturated, clean readable colour blocks.
Style: Chinese gongbi heavy-colour painting made into a modern game character design: confident black ink brush outlines of varying thickness, flat rich mineral pigments with a slight grainy pigment texture, very light cel shading.
Plain pale rice paper background with only a faint grey ink wash behind the feet, no scenery, no text, no signature, no seal, no border, no extra views.
Character: a Chinese wooden automaton gunner named Modou from an ancient architectural ruled-line painting, built of lacquered wooden blocks joined with visible mortise-and-tenon joints, medium height, slim and upright like a carpenter's square, warm honey-yellow wood with gold lacquer edges. Its head is a small square paper-and-wood lantern with a blank face and one glowing ink-black slit for eyes. Shoulders of stacked dougong brackets. One arm ends in a long multi-shot repeating crossbow of wood and bronze; the other arm is a heavy short bronze cannon held at the hip. A carpenter's ink-line reel and a folding wooden ruler hang at its belt, a thin black ink line trailing from the reel. Black ink glows in the cracks of the wood. No steam, no pipes, no metal gears, no rivets. Honey-yellow wood, gold and bronze dominate, gamboge-yellow tassels.
