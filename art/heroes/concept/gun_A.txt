Character concept art for a 2.5D side-scrolling action game, one character only, full body from head to feet, standing in a confident ready stance turned three-quarters toward the viewer, centered, filling the frame vertically.
Style: Chinese gongbi heavy-colour painting (gongbi zhongcai) made into a modern game character design: confident black ink brush outlines of varying thickness, flat rich mineral pigments (cinnabar red, azurite blue, malachite green, gamboge yellow, gold leaf) in clean readable colour blocks, very light cel shading, crisp and saturated. Strong, simple, instantly readable silhouette. Plain pale rice paper background with only a faint grey ink wash behind the feet, no scenery, no text, no seal, no signature, no border, no extra views.
Character: a lively clever young man engineer-gunner of Ming dynasty China, about 7 heads tall, slim build, a cocky smirk, black hair tied back, round brass goggles pushed up on his forehead. A pale off-white short robe with gamboge-yellow and gold trim, a brown leather belt hung with powder flasks and small tools, white trousers in wrapped boots. A long Ming matchlock fire-lance musket with gold fittings resting on his shoulder, and a compact repeating crossbow mechanism of wood and brass strapped to his left forearm. White, gamboge yellow and gold dominate.
