Style: Chinese gongbi heavy-colour painting with bold black ink brush outlines and flat mineral pigments.
Character: an unfinished painted swordsman of ancient China, the last figure a vanished master painter left half done: tall and lean, black topknot, stern face. His right half is fully painted in rich gongbi colour, a cinnabar-red robe with gold trim over black, a red scarf streaming; his left half is unfinished, only confident black ink outlines on blank paper with no colour inside, and the outline breaks into loose dry brush strokes and flying ink drops at the left sleeve and the hem. A thin straight jian sword held low whose blade is a single sweeping black brush stroke.
