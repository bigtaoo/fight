Character concept art for a 2.5D side-scrolling action game, one character only, full body from head to feet, in a dynamic ready stance turned three-quarters toward the viewer, centered, filling the frame vertically. A strong, simple, instantly readable silhouette, crisp and saturated, clean readable colour blocks.
Style: Chinese gongbi heavy-colour painting made into a modern game character design: confident black ink brush outlines of varying thickness, flat rich mineral pigments with a slight grainy pigment texture, very light cel shading.
Plain pale rice paper background with only a faint grey ink wash behind the feet, no scenery, no text, no signature, no seal, no border, no extra views.
Character: Modou, a living wooden automaton gunner that stepped out of an ancient Chinese ruled-line architectural painting: drawn with precise straight black ink lines as if with a ruler, a slim figure of joined wooden beams and blocks with mortise-and-tenon joints, warm honey-yellow wood with gold lacquer edges, small bronze corner fittings with cloud patterns. A wide carved wooden collar of stacked brackets. Its head is a carved wooden face like a Chinese opera mask, smooth and calm, with no eyes, one thin glowing line of black ink across them. A heavy bronze squatting-tiger cannon carried under one arm on a strap, a compact wooden repeating crossbow mounted on the other forearm, a folding carpenter's square ruler and an ink-line reel on its belt. Faint precise ruler guide lines float in the air around it. Not a robot, no gears, no pipes, no steam. Honey-yellow wood, gold and bronze dominate, gamboge-yellow tassels.
