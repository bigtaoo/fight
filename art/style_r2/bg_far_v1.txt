Paint on this blank sheet of paper one continuous Chinese ink wash landscape scroll, used as the far background layer of a side-scrolling action game. Distant misty mountain ranges in light grey ink washes across the whole width of the upper half, overlapping in two or three layers that get paler with distance; a few tiny pine trees on the nearest ridges; a soft mist band below the mountains. The lower third of the picture stays empty plain paper, where the characters will walk. Only light and mid grey tones with a little pale indigo, no pure black areas, no strong contrast. One single seamless painting: no grid, no panels, no lines, no frame, no border, no people, no animals, no buildings, no text, no seal.
