A Chinese blue-green landscape handscroll painting in the Song dynasty gongbi style, mineral pigments, fine ink outlines: a wooden plank road along a sheer cliff face across the whole width of the picture: horizontal planks laid on square wooden beams driven into the vertical rock, a simple thin wooden railing, a few small twisted pines clinging to the rock, the cliff face below the road. The road is broken in two places by wide gaps of blank unpainted paper, as if the painter had not finished, the planks ending in loose ink strokes at the gaps. Everything sits low in the picture, the road along one straight line near the bottom. The upper half of the picture is blank plain pale rice paper. Flat side view, wide panoramic composition, muted malachite green, azurite blue, ochre and ink grey, soft, no people, no animals, no text, no calligraphy, no seal, no signature, no frame, no border.
