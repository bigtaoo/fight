A Chinese blue-green landscape handscroll painting in the Song dynasty gongbi style, mineral pigments, fine ink outlines: a Buddhist mountain temple across the whole width of the picture: halls with sweeping grey tiled roofs and red pillars, ochre-yellow walls, a small bell tower, a stone stairway, stone lanterns, old twisted pine trees with flat dark green crowns, rocks. Everything stands on one straight ground line near the bottom edge. The upper half of the picture is blank plain pale rice paper. Flat side view, wide panoramic composition, muted malachite green, azurite blue, ochre and ink grey, soft, no people, no animals, no text, no calligraphy, no seal, no signature, no frame, no border.
