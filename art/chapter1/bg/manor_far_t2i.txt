A Chinese blue-green landscape handscroll painting in the style of the Song dynasty "A Thousand Li of Rivers and Mountains", mineral pigments on aged silk paper: the rooftops of a large walled mansion compound seen from the side, layered grey tiled roofs with upturned eaves among tall dark green trees and pale blossoming trees, gentle blue-green hills far behind with mist. The upper third is pale warm paper-white sky. The lower quarter is soft mist fading to plain pale paper along the bottom edge. Flat side view, horizontal composition, elegant, gentle, painterly, fine gongbi lines, no people, no buildings in front, no text, no calligraphy, no seal, no signature, no frame, no border.
