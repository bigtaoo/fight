A Chinese blue-green landscape handscroll painting in the Song dynasty gongbi style, mineral pigments, fine ink outlines: the furniture of a village main hall in a row across the whole width of the picture: in the centre a large four-panel standing folding screen with a dark red lacquered wooden frame, its four silk panels painted as one gongbi flower-and-bird painting of a flowering crabapple branch, lotus and bamboo with small birds and a golden pheasant on warm golden silk; on either side a plain square wooden table with a teapot and cups, two wooden armchairs, a tall ceramic jar, a low bench, a small altar shelf with an incense burner. Everything stands on one straight floor line near the bottom edge. The upper third of the picture is blank plain pale paper. Flat side view, wide panoramic composition, muted ochre, umber, cinnabar red and malachite green, soft, no people, no animals, no text, no calligraphy, no seal, no signature, no frame, no border.
