A Chinese blue-green landscape handscroll painting in the style of the Song dynasty "A Thousand Li of Rivers and Mountains", mineral pigments on aged silk paper: tall steep azurite-blue and malachite-green mountain peaks with fine ink outlines and gold highlights, a slender many-storeyed pagoda on a high ridge, a thin waterfall, drifting white clouds between the peaks. The upper third is pale warm paper-white sky. The lower quarter is soft white cloud fading to plain pale paper along the bottom edge. Flat side view, horizontal composition, elegant, gentle, painterly, fine gongbi lines, no people, no buildings in front, no text, no calligraphy, no seal, no signature, no frame, no border.
