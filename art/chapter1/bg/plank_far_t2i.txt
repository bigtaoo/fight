A Chinese blue-green landscape handscroll painting in the style of the Song dynasty "A Thousand Li of Rivers and Mountains", mineral pigments on aged silk paper: a deep narrow gorge between sheer vertical azurite-blue and malachite-green cliffs with fine ink outlines and thin gold highlights, a thin white waterfall, a tiny distant wooden plank road pinned along a far cliff face, soft white mist rising from below. The upper third is pale warm paper-white sky. The lower quarter is thick soft mist fading to plain pale paper along the bottom edge. Flat side view, horizontal composition, elegant, gentle, painterly, fine gongbi lines, no people, no buildings in front, no text, no calligraphy, no seal, no signature, no frame, no border.
