The unpainted tail section of an antique Chinese handscroll, flat front view: several tall sheets of aged paper mounted side by side, each a slightly different tone of cream, pale tea brown and pale grey-blue, with faint gold flecks, soft foxing stains, fine vertical seams between the sheets, very faint ghostly traces of a distant blue-green mountain fading out at the left. Nothing else painted. Calm, empty, elegant, no text, no calligraphy, no seal, no signature, no frame.
