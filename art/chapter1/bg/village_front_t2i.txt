A Chinese gongbi painting in Song dynasty style, mineral pigments, fine ink outlines and soft washes: three separate clumps close to the viewer standing on the bottom edge with wide blank gaps between them: tall reeds with feathery tops, a low mossy rock with green grass and small wild flowers, and a clump of slender bamboo shoots. Malachite green, pale ochre and grey ink, soft blurred edges. The rest of the picture is blank plain pale rice paper. Wide panoramic composition, flat side view, no people, no animals, no text, no calligraphy, no seal, no signature, no frame, no border.
