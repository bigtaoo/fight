A Chinese blue-green landscape handscroll painting in the style of the Song dynasty "A Thousand Li of Rivers and Mountains", mineral pigments on aged silk paper: a wide panoramic range of steep azurite-blue and malachite-green mountain peaks with fine ink outlines and thin gold highlights on the ridges, layered from pale distant peaks to nearer ones, soft white mist between them. The upper third is pale warm paper-white sky. The lower quarter is a calm pale jade river and soft mist with a few tiny distant sails, fading to plain pale paper along the bottom edge. Flat side view, horizontal composition, elegant, gentle, painterly, fine gongbi lines, no people close up, no buildings in front, no text, no calligraphy, no seal, no signature, no frame, no border.
