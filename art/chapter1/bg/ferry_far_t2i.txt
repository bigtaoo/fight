A Chinese blue-green landscape handscroll painting in the style of the Song dynasty "A Thousand Li of Rivers and Mountains", mineral pigments on aged silk paper: a very wide calm river with the far shore low and distant, gentle azurite-blue and malachite-green hills with gold highlights along the far shore, a few tiny sails, sandbars with reeds, soft white mist. The upper third is pale warm paper-white sky. The lower half is the wide calm pale jade river with fine ink ripple lines, fading to plain pale paper along the bottom edge. Flat side view, horizontal composition, elegant, gentle, painterly, fine gongbi lines, no people, no buildings in front, no text, no calligraphy, no seal, no signature, no frame, no border.
