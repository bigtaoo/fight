A Chinese blue-green landscape handscroll painting in the Song dynasty gongbi style, mineral pigments, fine ink outlines: the next stretch of the same riverside fishing village, a row of buildings along a pale earthen yard by a calm teal river across the whole width of the picture: a small two-storey wooden tea house with a cloth banner on a pole, thatched-roof huts with earthen walls, a wooden waterwheel, fishing nets hung to dry on bamboo poles, a woodpile, clay jars, a small shrine under a tiled roof, one large camphor tree with a round dark green crown in the middle, a few thin young trees. No willow trees. Everything stands on one straight ground line, the river along the bottom. The upper part of the picture is blank plain pale rice paper. Flat side view, wide panoramic composition, the buildings the same small size as in a wide village panorama, muted malachite green, ochre and ink grey, soft, no people, no animals, no text, no calligraphy, no seal, no signature, no frame, no border.
