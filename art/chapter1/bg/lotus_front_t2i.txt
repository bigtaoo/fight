A Chinese gongbi painting in Song dynasty style, mineral pigments, fine ink outlines and soft washes: three separate clumps close to the viewer standing on the bottom edge with wide blank gaps between them: one huge lotus leaf seen from the side on a tall stem, a pink lotus bud with two reeds, and a curled withered lotus leaf with a seed pod. Fine ink outlines, malachite green, rouge pink, soft gradation. The rest of the picture is blank plain pale silk. Wide panoramic composition, flat side view, no birds, no people, no text, no calligraphy, no seal, no signature, no frame, no border.
