A Chinese Song dynasty gongbi painting, mineral pigments on aged paper, fine ink outlines: the inside back wall of a modest village main hall seen straight on, filling the whole picture: plain warm ochre plaster walls between dark brown wooden pillars and beams, two tall latticed paper windows letting in soft light, a horizontal tie beam across the top, a few hanging dried herbs and a straw hat on a peg, a swept earthen floor along the bottom quarter fading to plain pale paper along the bottom edge. Calm, quiet, softly lit, muted ochre, umber, pale green and ink grey. Flat side view, horizontal composition, no furniture in the middle, no people, no animals, no text, no calligraphy, no seal, no signature, no frame, no border.
