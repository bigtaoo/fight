A Chinese gongbi painting in Song dynasty style, mineral pigments, fine ink outlines and soft washes: a bamboo grove and garden rocks across the whole width of the picture: clusters of slender green bamboo stems with fine pointed leaves in groups, tall pierced blue-grey Taihu scholar's rocks with holes, smaller round stones, a few orchids and low grasses at their feet, on warm golden-beige silk. Everything stands on one straight ground line near the bottom edge. The upper third of the picture is blank plain pale silk. Flat side view, wide panoramic composition, no birds, no insects, no people, no text, no calligraphy, no seal, no signature, no frame, no border.
