A Chinese blue-green landscape handscroll painting in the Song dynasty gongbi style, mineral pigments, fine ink outlines: a small stream crossing a river village across the whole width of the picture: a humpbacked stone footbridge over the stream in the middle, a plain wooden plank bridge with a bamboo handrail further along, drooping willow trees with yellow-green leaves on both banks, reeds and cattails along the water, a few mossy stones, a thatched hut half hidden behind the willows at one end, a fishing pole leaning on the bank. Everything stands on one straight ground line near the bottom edge. The upper half of the picture is blank plain pale rice paper. Flat side view, wide panoramic composition, muted malachite green, azurite blue, ochre and ink grey, soft, no people, no animals, no text, no calligraphy, no seal, no signature, no frame, no border.
