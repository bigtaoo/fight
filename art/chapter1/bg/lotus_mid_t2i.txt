A Chinese gongbi painting in Song dynasty style, mineral pigments, fine ink outlines and soft washes: a lotus pond across the whole width of the picture: big round lotus leaves in malachite and olive green, some flat on the water and some held up high on tall slender stems at different heights, pink lotus flowers in bloom and in bud, a seed pod, a few reeds and arrowhead leaves, pale azurite blue water ripples drawn in fine lines along the bottom, on warm golden-beige silk. Everything grows from one straight water line near the bottom edge. The upper third of the picture is blank plain pale silk. Flat side view, wide panoramic composition, no birds, no insects, no people, no text, no calligraphy, no seal, no signature, no frame, no border.
