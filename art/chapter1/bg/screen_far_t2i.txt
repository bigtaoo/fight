A Chinese gongbi flower-and-bird painting in Song dynasty imperial academy style, mineral pigments on warm golden-beige silk: a soft hazy background of the silk itself with faint pale distant branches of plum blossom and a large pale round moon, gentle washes of pale gold and pale green, very delicate, mostly empty silk. The lower quarter fades to plain pale silk. Flat side view, horizontal composition, elegant, no birds, no people, no text, no calligraphy, no seal, no signature, no frame, no border.
