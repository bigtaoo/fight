A Chinese gongbi painting in Song dynasty style, mineral pigments, fine ink outlines and soft washes: large blossoming branches across the whole width of the picture growing from a row of pierced blue-grey scholar's rocks along the bottom edge: pink and white tree peonies with big petals, white magnolia, red camellias, slender green leaves, every petal with fine ink outlines and soft colour gradation, on warm golden-beige silk. Everything grows from one straight line near the bottom edge. The upper half of the picture is blank plain pale silk. Flat side view, wide panoramic composition, no birds, no insects, no people, no text, no calligraphy, no seal, no signature, no frame, no border.
