A Chinese gongbi painting in Song dynasty style, mineral pigments, fine ink outlines and soft washes: three separate clumps close to the viewer standing on the bottom edge with wide blank gaps between them: a few slender bamboo stems with fine leaves, a low branch of red plum blossom, and a clump of orchid leaves with small flowers. Fine ink outlines, malachite green, rouge red, soft gradation. The rest of the picture is blank plain pale silk. Wide panoramic composition, flat side view, no birds, no people, no text, no calligraphy, no seal, no signature, no frame, no border.
