A Chinese blue-green landscape handscroll painting in the Song dynasty gongbi style, mineral pigments, fine ink outlines, seen exactly from the side with no perspective: a river shore of a fishing village in a row along the bottom edge across the whole width of the picture: a low grey stone embankment with stone steps down to the water, big flat dark washing stones with a wooden tub and laundry paddle, tall bamboo poles with lines of pale blue and indigo cloth hung to dry, two small wooden fishing boats pulled up on the shingle, coiled fishing nets on a rack, clumps of reeds, a willow tree. Everything stands on one straight ground line near the bottom edge. The upper half of the picture is blank plain pale rice paper with no mountains and no water. Flat side view, wide panoramic composition, strong ink outlines, muted malachite green, azurite blue, indigo, ochre and ink grey, no people, no animals, no text, no calligraphy, no seal, no signature, no frame, no border.
