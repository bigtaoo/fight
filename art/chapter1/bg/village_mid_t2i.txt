A Chinese blue-green landscape handscroll painting in the Song dynasty gongbi style, mineral pigments, fine ink outlines: a long riverside fishing village in a row along the bottom edge of the picture across its whole width: thatched-roof wooden huts with earthen walls, a small arched wooden footbridge, drooping willow trees with yellow-green leaves, a bamboo fence, fishing nets hung to dry on poles, a few stacked baskets and firewood. Everything stands on one straight ground line at the bottom edge. The upper half of the picture is blank plain pale rice paper. Flat side view, wide panoramic composition, muted malachite green, ochre and ink grey, soft, no people, no animals, no text, no calligraphy, no seal, no signature, no frame, no border.
