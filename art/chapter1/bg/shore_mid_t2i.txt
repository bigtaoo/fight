A Chinese blue-green landscape handscroll painting in the Song dynasty gongbi style, mineral pigments, fine ink outlines: a wide pebble river shore of a fishing village across the whole width of the picture: flat grey washing stones at the water's edge with a wooden tub and a laundry paddle, bamboo poles with pale blue cloth hung to dry, two small wooden fishing boats pulled up on the shingle, coiled nets, a few reeds and a lone willow, a low stone embankment wall with steps at one end. Everything stands on one straight ground line near the bottom edge. The upper half of the picture is blank plain pale rice paper. Flat side view, wide panoramic composition, muted malachite green, azurite blue, ochre and ink grey, soft, no people, no animals, no text, no calligraphy, no seal, no signature, no frame, no border.
