A Chinese blue-green landscape handscroll painting in the Song dynasty gongbi style, mineral pigments, fine ink outlines: a river ferry crossing across the whole width of the picture: a long wooden pier on stilts over the water, a small thatched ferry pavilion with a wooden bench, two small wooden ferry boats with bamboo awnings moored to posts, a tall bamboo pole with a cloth flag, a willow tree, reed beds. Everything stands on one straight waterline near the bottom edge, calm teal water below. The upper half of the picture is blank plain pale rice paper. Flat side view, wide panoramic composition, muted malachite green, azurite blue, ochre and ink grey, soft, no people, no animals, no text, no calligraphy, no seal, no signature, no frame, no border.
