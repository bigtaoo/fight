A Chinese gongbi painting in Song dynasty style, mineral pigments, fine ink outlines and soft washes: one enormous flowering crabapple tree branch growing in from the left and stretching horizontally across the whole width of the picture: thick gnarled dark brown boughs that fork into two long level limbs at different heights, covered in clusters of pink and white crabapple blossoms with red buds and fresh green leaves, every petal with fine ink outlines and soft colour gradation, on warm golden-beige silk. The trunk rises from a blue-grey scholar's rock on the bottom edge at the left. The upper third of the picture is blank plain pale silk. Flat side view, wide panoramic composition, no birds, no insects, no people, no text, no calligraphy, no seal, no signature, no frame, no border.
