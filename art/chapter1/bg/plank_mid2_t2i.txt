A Chinese blue-green landscape handscroll painting in the Song dynasty gongbi style, mineral pigments, fine ink outlines, seen exactly from the side like an architectural elevation with no perspective: a long low band of grey-blue cliff rock running straight across the whole width of the picture along the bottom edge, and on top of it a perfectly level wooden plank road running in one straight horizontal line from the left edge to the right edge: flat planks resting on square wooden beams driven into the rock, a thin wooden railing of posts and rope. The road is broken in two places by wide gaps of blank unpainted paper, the planks ending in loose ink strokes. A few small twisted pines cling to the rock. The upper half of the picture is blank plain pale rice paper with no mountains. Flat orthographic side view, wide panoramic composition, muted malachite green, azurite blue, ochre and ink grey, soft, no people, no animals, no text, no calligraphy, no seal, no signature, no frame, no border.
