A Chinese blue-green landscape handscroll painting in the Song dynasty gongbi style, mineral pigments, fine ink outlines: the front of a grand old mansion seen straight from the side across the whole width of the picture: a long whitewashed courtyard wall with a grey tiled coping, in the middle a tall gatehouse with a sweeping grey tiled roof and a pair of closed vermilion wooden doors with gold studs, three low stone steps, two empty square stone pedestals on either side of the gate, a pine and a flowering tree peeking over the wall. Everything stands on one straight ground line near the bottom edge. The upper part of the picture is blank plain pale rice paper. No lions, no statues. Flat side view, wide panoramic composition, muted malachite green, azurite blue, ochre and ink grey, soft, no people, no animals, no text, no calligraphy, no seal, no signature, no frame, no border.
