A Chinese gongbi painting in Song dynasty style, mineral pigments, fine ink outlines and soft washes: three separate pieces close to the viewer standing on the bottom edge with wide blank gaps between them: the base of a dark brown wooden pillar on a round stone plinth, a low wooden stool with a basket, and a large glazed water jar with a ladle. Umber, ochre and grey ink, soft blurred edges. The rest of the picture is blank plain pale paper. Wide panoramic composition, flat side view, no people, no animals, no text, no calligraphy, no seal, no signature, no frame, no border.
