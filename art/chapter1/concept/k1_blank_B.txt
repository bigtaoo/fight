Wide 16:9 key frame from a 2.5D side-scrolling action game set inside a living Chinese handscroll painting. The screen is almost entirely blank warm cream xuan paper with soft fibre texture, framed at the very top and bottom by thin strips of pale blue-green silk scroll mounting. Left of centre a swordsman has just been painted into existence by a single sweeping dry-brush stroke (feibai): a tall slim figure seen from the side, wide conical bamboo rain hat worn low hiding his face, cinnabar-red long robe whose torn hem streams backward, black inner clothes and boots, a long straight sword held low trailing behind him. His lower body and robe tail still dissolve into the raw brushstroke and flying ink droplets, as if the paint is still wet. He is the only saturated colour on the page. To the right, ahead of him, the landscape is drawing itself in: first faint grey ink outlines of distant mountains, a riverbank path and a few willow trees, and just beyond him the first washes of mineral azurite blue and malachite green are seeping into the outlines. Further right the paper is still empty. A floating calligraphy brush tip glints with gold near the hero. Elegant, quiet, minimal, lots of empty space, Song dynasty blue-green landscape painting style, gongbi line quality, no UI, no text, no frame other than the silk mounting.
