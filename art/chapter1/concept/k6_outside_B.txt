Cinematic final scene of a game chapter, very wide horizontal 2:1 composition. A Chinese blue-green landscape handscroll painting fills the frame, hung up and lit from behind by warm candlelight, so the paper glows amber and the painted mountains, river and tiny village show through as translucent silhouettes; the silk mounting and wooden roller are visible along the top edge where the scroll is held up. On the far side of the paper, the dark shadow of a tall robed figure stands with a candle, its outline soft and blurred through the paper, face unreadable, much larger than everything in the painting; its hand shadow reaches toward the scroll. Inside the painting, at the bottom on a small river path, three tiny painted figures look up: a swordsman in a red robe and conical bamboo hat, a broad stone lion with a blue mane, and a wooden puppet in a winged black hat. Glowing paper fibres, ink and mineral pigment texture, mysterious, quiet, awe, not horror, no text, no signature, no seals.
