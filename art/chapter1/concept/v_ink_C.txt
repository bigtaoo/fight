Character concept sheet for a 2.5D action game set inside a living Chinese painting: three villagers from a Song dynasty blue-green landscape scroll who have been corrupted by spilled ink, standing side by side in full body side-3/4 view on plain warm cream xuan paper, evenly spaced, each about the same height. They are painted figures, not monsters from horror: they keep the shapes and silhouettes of ordinary painted villagers, drawn in fine gongbi ink line style, but their bodies have turned into flat grey and black ink wash that is wet and running, ink dripping from their sleeves, hems and fingertips and pooling at their feet, edges bleeding into the paper like ink on damp rice paper. Their faces are blank smooth ink with no features. Each keeps exactly one small patch of its original bright mineral colour, everything else grey-black ink: 1) left, a fisherman wearing a wide straw rain cape (suoyi) and a conical hat, holding a long bamboo fishing pole like a spear, the straw cape keeps its malachite green; 2) middle, a stocky woodcutter carrying a big bundle of firewood on his back and an axe in his hand, his sash keeps its ochre-yellow; 3) right, a slim washerwoman holding a wooden laundry paddle like a club, a wooden basin at her hip, her headscarf keeps its azurite blue. Hunched, menacing poses ready to attack, readable silhouettes. Clean background, no text.
